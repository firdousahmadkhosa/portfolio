Before you get in touch

Are you available right now?
Yes. I'm available to interview immediately and can start on short notice, for full-time roles or contract work. I'm based in Lahore on GMT+5, which overlaps comfortably with the Gulf, Europe and most of Asia.
Do you work remotely, or would you relocate?
Both. Most of my recent work has been remote for clients in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Oman, so I'm used to distributed delivery. I'm also open to relocating for the right role, including within the GCC.
Can you take over an existing codebase?
That's a large part of what I do. Several of my engagements started with a system someone else built that had outgrown its architecture — slow queries, no tenancy model, manual releases. I read the code first, tell you honestly what I find, and prioritise the changes that actually move the needle.
What size teams have you led?
Up to eight engineers, at Beyond Technologies, where I owned architecture, sprint planning and release quality alongside writing code. I run code review and mentor junior engineers rather than managing from a distance.
How do you price project work?
After a short discovery conversation I'll scope the work and quote either a fixed price per milestone or a monthly rate, depending on how well-defined the project is. You'll know the number before anything starts, and I'll tell you if I think the scope is wrong.
